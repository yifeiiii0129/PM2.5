Understanding the columns

ColumnMeaning
year / ageGroupExposure and baseline-mortality year / age range. City files cover 25+ throughout.
currentDeathsEstimated annual PM₂.₅-attributable NCD+LRI deaths.
avoidableDeathsEstimated annual deaths avoidable under the 5 μg/m³ scenario.
who5DeathsCurrent attributable deaths minus avoidable deaths: the remaining modeled burden.
avoidableShareA fraction: avoidableDeaths / currentDeaths. 0.8 means 80%. This is not the share of all deaths.
…RatePer100kAnnual deaths per 100,000 people in the corresponding age group. Crude, not age-standardized.
…Low / …HighSource GEMM parameter-based bounds, not full uncertainty intervals. Blank for newly merged age groups.
populationDenominator / windowPopulation25PlusThe population used for the age-specific country rate / adult city-window rate.
totalPopulation / windowPopulationTotalTotal modeled population of the country / window, including younger people.
citySourcePopulationPopulation in the city source database; used for eligibility, not for calculating the displayed mortality rate.
areaWeightedPm25UgM3 / populationWeightedPm25UgM3Country area-weighted / city population-weighted annual mean PM₂.₅, in μg/m³.
windowAreaKm2 / windowCells / validPm25CellsModeled area, number of cells, and number with valid PM₂.₅ support.
fractionalLandAreaKm2 / fractionalAreaCells / fractionalPopulationCellsCountry modeled area and counts of cells allocated fractionally at boundaries.
ageAggregation / intervalNoteCountry total versus summary age group, and explanation of available parameter bounds.

Blank values mean unavailable, not zero. Do not sum country totals and their city windows. See Methods for calculation and geographic limitations.

Using and crediting these results

Identify the project, selected year, age group, geographic scope and 5 μg/m³ scenario when sharing results. Link to this website and include the date accessed. These estimates use GEMM with GBD baseline mortality; they are not direct GBD PM₂.₅ mortality outputs.

Input references and source licenses are listed on the Methods page.
