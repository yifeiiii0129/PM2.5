PM₂.₅ Health Benefits

Making cleaner air's health benefits visible

Explore what reaching the WHO annual PM₂.₅ guideline could mean for adult mortality in countries and areas around cities.

Why this tool exists

Pollution concentrations tell one part of the story. This project connects outdoor PM₂.₅ exposure with estimated health impacts, helping clean-air advocates and decision-makers understand the potential benefits of reducing exposure.

The site provides estimates for 2020–2023 using a shared research workflow. It supports exploration and comparison; it does not prescribe a particular policy or predict how quickly benefits would occur.
